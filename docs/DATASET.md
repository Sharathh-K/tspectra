# Dataset

Source: Kaggle "Beyond Visible Spectrum: AI for Agriculture" (P1).

- UAV hyperspectral imagery of winter wheat, 25 Apr - 18 May 2018
- Sensor: UHD 185 Firefly, 450-950 nm, 4 nm spectral resolution
- Output: 1000x1000 image, 125 bands, ~2 cm/pixel, altitude 30 m

Place the downloaded files in `data/raw/` (not tracked by git).
